# Crimson Court — Asset Art Direction

## Visual identity
Black marble and obsidian architecture, blood-red crystal, living crimson thorn growth, pale stone accents, deep burgundy shadows, red-moon illumination. The faction must read as aristocratic Blood Elves rather than Inferno/demon imagery.

## Town screen
- VCMI target background: 800x374.
- Strong depth layers: foreground court/bridge, midground buildable palace district, distant bloodwood and towers.
- Keep structure footprints readable; avoid excessive full-screen red bloom.
- Empty/unbuilt town must still look intentional.
- Buildings should visually upgrade rather than become unrelated silhouettes.

## Creature language
T1 Veinling/Bloodbound: lean court thralls, pale/ashen skin, restrained crimson accents; upgrade gains disciplined armour and stronger silhouette.
T2 Thorn Dancer/Crimson Dancer: agile Blood Elf duelists using thorn/ribbon-like weapon language; upgrade becomes more ceremonial and lethal, not bulkier.

Battle animation production must cover at minimum idle, movement, hit/defend, death and relevant melee/ranged groups before conversion can be considered complete.

## Heroes
Vaelor: Bloodlord, severe martial aristocrat, dark hair, pale skin, restrained red armour/crystal.
Aveline: Sanguine Seer, arcane aristocrat, pale/white hair acceptable, mystical blood-red accents without gore.

Portraits must remain legible at Heroes III UI scale; facial silhouette and class read are more important than micro-detail.

## Siege
Use the same black-stone/red-crystal construction language as the town. All CRSG prefix states must remain geometrically aligned across intact/damaged/destroyed variants.

## Production status discipline
Concept/reference imagery is **not** a VCMI asset and must not advance a manifest job beyond CONCEPT. Generated or painted source art becomes SOURCE_READY only when the exact resource file exists in the repository. Converted animation/UI resources become CONVERTED only after conversion. Only a successful local VCMI 1.7.5 render/test may set VALIDATED_VCMI.

No generated progress numbers or illustrative checklist on a concept board are project status evidence; machine-readable manifests and validator output are authoritative.
