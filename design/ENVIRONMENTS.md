# Environment & Town Art Direction

The goal is not to recolor existing terrain. Each realm needs a distinct silhouette, prop language and navigation feel while preserving Heroes III readability.

## Bloodwood — Crimson Court
**Palette concept:** black bark, dark earth, crimson foliage, pale stone, restrained red glow.
**Landmarks:** bloodwood groves, thorn arches, moon pools, black-marble ruins.
**Town:** a vertical black-marble court grown around an immense crimson tree.
**Town-building language:** living branches physically integrate with aristocratic architecture.
**Grail:** Heart of the Red Moon.

Signature town structures:
- Court of Veins
- Thorn Gallery
- Sanguine Archive
- Moonlit Menagerie
- Scarlet Conservatory
- Hall of the First Chalice

## Abyssal Shore — Abyss
**Palette concept:** black sand, cold blue-green water, bone-white fossils, faint bioluminescence.
**Landmarks:** trenches, impossible shells, drowned columns, giant ribs.
**Town:** a partially submerged city inside a cavern whose water hides something colossal.
**Town-building language:** drowned cyclopean stone, shell-like openings and architecture that appears too old for its inhabitants.
**Grail:** The Unfathomed Eye.

Signature town structures:
- Hall Below
- Blind Observatory
- Maw Chapel
- Archive of Drowned Names
- Leviathan Ossuary
- Well Without Bottom

## Dreamscape — Veil
**Palette concept:** moonlit violet, cobalt, silver, luminous flora.
**Landmarks:** inverted waterfalls, floating stones, impossible stairs, mirror pools.
**Town:** several floating islands under an enormous moon.
**Town-building language:** graceful structures whose geometry subtly contradicts perspective.
**Grail:** The Lucid Moon.

Signature town structures:
- Palace of Unfinished Dreams
- Observatory of Sleep
- Garden of Second Memories
- Door of Elsewhere
- Hall of Lucidity
- Nightmare Loom

## Hollow Marches — Hollow
**Palette concept:** ash-grey grass, candle gold, black roses, fog, muted burgundy.
**Landmarks:** abandoned processional roads, empty carriages, bell towers, masked statues.
**Town:** a silent royal capital prepared forever for a coronation that never occurs.
**Town-building language:** late-gothic court architecture, endless candles and conspicuously empty spaces.
**Grail:** The Empty Throne.

Signature town structures:
- Court of Broken Oaths
- Hall of Masks
- Black Rose Cloister
- Belfry of the Last Hour
- Banquet of No Guests
- Nameless Coronation Hall

## Starfallen Lands — Starfall
**Palette concept:** pale stone, midnight blue, gold, silver soil, restrained prismatic highlights.
**Landmarks:** impact craters, monoliths, concentric stone circles, celestial flora.
**Town:** a sacred concentric city around an ancient impact crater.
**Town-building language:** antique temple forms combined with impossible rings, eyes and celestial geometry — explicitly mythic, not mechanical or sci-fi.
**Grail:** Throne Beyond the Firmament.

Signature town structures:
- Temple of Seven Halos
- Choir of Orbits
- House of the First Messenger
- Constellation Court
- Sanctuary of Falling Light
- Gate of the Firmament

## Readability constraints
- Walkable and blocked tiles must remain visually obvious.
- Interactive objects need stronger silhouettes than decoration.
- Decorative glow must not obscure roads, resources, mines or armies.
- Battlefields inherit environmental identity without reducing hex/unit readability.
- Final assets should target VCMI-supported PNG workflows where practical.
