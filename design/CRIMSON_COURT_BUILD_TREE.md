# Crimson Court — Building Tree v0.1

## Economic / universal line
Village Hall -> Town Hall -> City Hall -> Capitol
Fort -> Citadel -> Castle
Marketplace -> Resource Silo
Tavern -> Brotherhood-style morale structure (final faction name pending)
Blacksmith
Mage Guild I -> II -> III -> IV -> V

## Dwelling progression
**Vein House** -> **Vein House, Consecrated** (T1)
- prerequisite: Fort

**Thorn Gallery** -> **Crimson Gallery** (T2)
- prerequisite: Vein House

**Gorewing Roost** -> **Bloodwing Roost** (T3)
- prerequisite: Thorn Gallery

**Hall of Veins** -> **Oracle Hall** (T4)
- prerequisite: Mage Guild I + Gorewing Roost

**Scarlet Lodge** -> **Bloodstalker Lodge** (T5)
- prerequisite: Hall of Veins + Blacksmith

**Sanguine Palace** -> **Crimson Archon Palace** (T6)
- prerequisite: Scarlet Lodge + City Hall + Mage Guild II

**Heart Aviary** -> **Eternal Heart Aviary** (T7)
- prerequisite: Sanguine Palace + Castle + Mage Guild III

## Faction specials
### Court of Veins
Prereq: Town Hall + Mage Guild I.
Unlocks Blood Rite system. It does not itself grant a free combat buff.

### Scarlet Conservatory
Prereq: Citadel + Thorn Gallery.
Growth-oriented building; exact bonus test-gated.

### Hall of the First Chalice
Prereq: Court of Veins + Mage Guild II.
Hero/faction-lore building. Any repeatable effect must have a strict cap.

### Moonlit Menagerie
Prereq: Gorewing Roost + Scarlet Conservatory.
Narrow creature-support effect, likely T3/T5 interaction.

### Thorn Tribunal
Prereq: Fort + Blacksmith.
Town-defense identity without duplicating Fortress raw defense.

### Red Moon Observatory
Prereq: Mage Guild III + Hall of the First Chalice.
Sanguine Seer / blood-spell support.

### Grail — Heart of the Red Moon
Standard Grail construction path plus faction-specific bounded effect.

## Build-order constraints
- T6 cannot be a trivial week-one purchase.
- T7 requires meaningful economy/magic/fortification investment.
- Blood Rite access competes with dwelling tempo.
- Growth building arrives early enough to matter but costs tempo/resources.
- No unique building generates uncapped permanent hero stats.
