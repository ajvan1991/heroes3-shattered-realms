# Crimson Court Heroes

The regular Crimson Court roster follows the standard Heroes III town pattern: **16 regular heroes — 8 Bloodlords and 8 Sanguine Seers**.

The canonical regular roster, specialties and roles are maintained in:
- `design/CRIMSON_COURT_FINAL_ROSTER.md`
- `shattered-realms/Content/config/shattered-realms/crimson/heroes.staging.json`

## Campaign / special reserve
These concepts are intentionally **not** part of the normal tavern pool:
- Corvane — campaign Bloodlord candidate
- Nerezza — campaign Bloodlord candidate
- Orlanna — campaign Sanguine Seer candidate
- Morcant — campaign Sanguine Seer candidate

They may later use `special: true` and appear through authored maps/campaign content.

## Roster rule
Do not expand the regular town roster above 8 Might + 8 Magic merely to preserve old concepts. New narrative heroes belong in the campaign/special pool unless a regular hero is deliberately replaced.
