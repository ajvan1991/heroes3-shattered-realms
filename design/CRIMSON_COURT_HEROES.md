# Crimson Court — Hero Roster

Crimson Court ships with **20 heroes**: 10 Bloodlords and 10 Sanguine Seers.

Creature specialties apply to both the base creature and its upgrade. Exact Heroes III-style level scaling will be validated in VCMI implementation.

### Biographies
**Vaelor:** Once master of the lowest ritual houses, Vaelor believes nobility begins with surviving what others cannot. His initiates follow him because he never asks them to bleed first.

**Seris:** Seris won her title in the Thorn Gallery without spilling a drop of her own blood. The court still debates whether that feat was elegance, sorcery, or cruelty.

**Khaeren:** Khaeren spent years among the high roosts and learned the Gorewings' hunting calls. They descend for him even when no keeper can compel them.

**Maelira:** The Scarlet Huntress Maelira has never abandoned a marked quarry. Entire border courts have surrendered after finding her crimson sigil on their gates.

**Othrys:** Othrys is old enough to remember when the Sanguine Palace had no roof. Younger nobles mock his patience only once.

**Rhaevan:** Rhaevan guards the forbidden rites from which the Blood Phoenix was born. He considers the creature neither beast nor miracle, but a debt the court has yet to repay.

**Thalia Veyn:** Thalia treats battle as formal argument. Every charge is a proposition, every retreat a concession, and every dead enemy a conclusion.

**Corvane:** Corvane survived three succession wars by refusing glorious victories in favor of positions that could not be broken. His soldiers have adopted the same stubborn discipline.

**Ilyr Thorn:** Ilyr studies the instant when pain becomes resolve. His armies are infamous for becoming most dangerous precisely when their enemies believe them defeated.

**Nerezza:** Nerezza maps roads by moonlight and remembers every hidden crossing in the Bloodwood. Her columns often arrive before scouts have finished reporting their departure.

## Sanguine Seers — Magic

| Hero | Specialty | Design |
|---|---|---|
| **Aveline** | Hemomancers | Hemomancer/Vein Oracle specialist; level-scaled Attack/Defense. |
| **Sevrin** | Bloodied spellcraft | Improves one faction blood spell's effect, not its sacrifice bypass. |
| **Miraleth** | Blood Rite | Reduces a specific Rite's opportunity cost within the global minimum. |
| **Caelis** | Intelligence | Intelligence specialty using established scaling. |
| **Vespera** | Sorcery | Sorcery specialty using established scaling. |
| **Elyss Vane** | Mysticism | Mysticism specialty using established scaling. |
| **Orlanna** | Vein Oracle casting | Improves a bounded Vein Oracle spell effect/cast count after implementation review. |
| **Saereth** | Scarlet Mark | Enhances Quarry Mark duration/utility without increasing multiplicative damage. |
| **Lysandra Noct** | Crimson Divination | Improves the temporary next-spell payoff of the class passive, not the sacrifice floor. |
| **Morcant** | Blood Phoenix rite | Supports Blood Phoenixes through a narrow magic-oriented specialty; does not grant extra rebirths. |

### Biographies
**Aveline:** Aveline reads blood as other scholars read ink. She claims every living creature carries a history of choices in its veins, though she refuses to say what she has read in her own.

**Sevrin:** Sevrin's rituals are frighteningly precise. He despises waste and teaches that a single deliberate drop is worth more than a basin spilled without purpose.

**Miraleth:** Miraleth catalogued the oldest surviving Blood Rites and discovered that several had been mistranslated for centuries. The nobles who profited from those errors have never forgiven her.

**Caelis:** Caelis remembers every formula he has encountered and most conversations he wishes he could forget. His apprentices complain that ignorance is impossible in his presence.

**Vespera:** Vespera believes magic should end a battle before steel understands it has begun. Her demonstrations have made this opinion difficult to dispute.

**Elyss Vane:** Elyss spends long nights in silent meditation beside the ritual channels. By dawn, the magic others have exhausted often seems to have returned to her.

**Orlanna:** Orlanna was blinded during her initiation and emerged claiming she could finally see. The Vein Oracles accepted her before the court physicians had finished their examination.

**Saereth:** Saereth studies the invisible bonds between hunter and quarry. Once he names a target, every path on the battlefield seems to bend toward it.

**Lysandra Noct:** Lysandra does not predict the future; she selects which future is worth bleeding for. Even other Seers find the distinction unsettling.

**Morcant:** Morcant has spent decades studying the ritual heart at the center of every Blood Phoenix. He insists rebirth is not immortality, but memory given shape.
