# Spell Art / VFX Matrix

All 12 spells require original spellbook, scroll, active-effect, immunity and scenario-bonus icons plus battle VFX before runtime activation.

| Spell | Icon motif | Cast / affect animation |
|---|---|---|
| Sever the Formation | broken three-point halo | thin wind-lines cut a geometric link; brief fractured halo |
| Clear Mind | open silver eye in clean sky | translucent ring passes outward from head/upper body |
| Unbound Step | winged footprint | pale wind ribbon unwraps from feet |
| Grounding Sigil | stone circle pierced by falling star | low earthen glyph locks to ground; no chain visuals |
| Mortal Anchor | iron-black anchor over fading halo | dark stone weight briefly descends behind target |
| Burden of Stone | cracked crown under a rock | dust/stone weight settles over shoulders |
| Brand of Frailty | narrow ember brand | small red-orange sigil burns then dims |
| Scorch the Oath | burning broken wax seal | wax-seal rune cracks in a brief flame line |
| Flare of Waking | sunrise through a closed eye | sharp warm flash opens into a thin eye glyph |
| Quiet the Blood | crimson droplet beneath blue ripple | cool ring compresses red motes toward target |
| Wash Away | white shell in circular wave | clean water spiral lifts removable effect motes away |
| Veil of Still Water | vertical mirror of water | transparent water plane forms briefly before target |

## VFX constraints
- 0.4–1.2 second readable effect.
- Avoid full-screen flashes except future level-5 spells.
- Counter VFX communicates suppression/cleansing rather than destruction.
- Effects must remain readable against all five new terrain palettes.
- No modern neon/UI aesthetic.

| Spell | Icon motif | Cast / affect animation |
|---|---|---|
| Leyline Convergence | four interlocked elemental lines around a pale crystal | thin ley lines briefly cross the whole battlefield; restrained and translucent |
| Echo of the Fallen | translucent warrior silhouette reflected in water | ghostlike memory silhouettes rise behind the target, then fold inward as a defensive shimmer |

Global NO_TARGET effects must remain short and non-obstructive. Their animation must communicate battlefield-wide scope without covering hexes or unit silhouettes.
