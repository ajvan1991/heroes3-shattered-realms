# Universal Spell Matchup Matrix

The Shattered Realms spell set is universal. Matchup notes describe where a spell becomes especially valuable; they do not restrict who can learn or cast it.

| Spell | General use | Particularly useful against |
|---|---|---|
| Sever the Formation | disrupt tough/fast stacks and defensive clusters | Starfall formations, high-defense elites, bodyguard play |
| Clear Mind | protect a key living stack from mind control | Veil control, classic Berserk/Hypnotize-style threats |
| Unbound Step | reposition, reach shooters, escape zones | slow armies, kiting, Abyss/Veil mobility races |
| Grounding Sigil | slow a dangerous diver or flier | fast T6/T7s, Abyss repositioning, Veil mobility |
| Mortal Anchor | anti-sustain slot; exact runtime gated | resurrection, regeneration, Crimson/Phoenix sustain |
| Burden of Stone | reduce melee/ranged threat output | Bloodied attackers, high-Attack elites, classic glass cannons |
| Brand of Frailty | open a durable target to focus fire | Hollow/Starfall tanks, Armorer-heavy armies |
| Scorch the Oath | general temporary-buff pressure slot | Hollow Oaths and buff-stacked units |
| Flare of Waking | control recovery plus tempo | Veil sleep/dream control, classic mind effects |
| Quiet the Blood | mild universal offensive suppression | Crimson Bloodied/Blood Command burst turns |
| Wash Away | general cleanse/dispel slot | faction marks, curses, ordinary negative magic |
| Veil of Still Water | general anti-burst defense | Crimson focus fire, Starfall burst, high-damage neutrals |
| Leyline Convergence | symmetrical magic-stability tool | spell-heavy matchups of any faction |
| Echo of the Fallen | casualty-dependent comeback defense | attrition battles, neutral clearing, snowball pressure |
| Fateful Exchange | trade one blessing and one curse | heavily enchanted stacks of any faction |
| Hour of Silence | symmetrical spell-tempo tax | high-mana spell chains and magic-heavy heroes |

## Distribution
- Existing H3/HotA factions: eligible through normal spell availability once compatibility mapping is enabled.
- Shattered Realms factions: eligible with faction-specific gain weights for balance/flavor.
- Hero class does not grant ownership of a universal spell.
- RMG may place scrolls/shrines for these spells using ordinary rarity rules.
- Blood Rites are separate: those are Crimson class/faction actions and are not part of this universal spell pool.
