# Global specialty distribution guardrails
- Every faction has recognizable creature specialists.
- T7 specialists receive weaker percentage scaling than low-tier specialists where required by balance.
- Skill specialists use familiar Heroes III logic.
- New faction-mechanic specialists improve reliability or tactical options rather than deleting the mechanic's downside.
- No regular hero begins with an exclusive combination that trivially guarantees a dominant week-one strategy.
