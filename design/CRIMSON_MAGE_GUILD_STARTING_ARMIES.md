# Crimson Court — Mage Guild & Starting Army Pass

## Mage Guild identity
Crimson Court has Mage Guild levels I–V. It should lean toward tactical buffs/debuffs and blood-themed control, but it must still draw from the normal spell ecosystem.

### New-spell weighting
When the 16 Shattered Realms spells are activated:
- Quiet the Blood: low native weight (counter spell; useful but not faction signature)
- Wash Away: medium
- Veil of Still Water: medium
- Brand of Frailty: medium
- Mortal Anchor: low
- Clear Mind: medium
- Global four: low across all factions
- Blood-signature spell(s) created later: high Crimson weight

No town receives guaranteed access to a direct counter for its own core mechanic.

## Starting army patterns

### Bloodlords
A. 18–24 Veinlings + 3–5 Thorn Dancers
B. 20–27 Veinlings
C. 15–20 Veinlings + 4–6 Thorn Dancers

### Sanguine Seers
A. 16–22 Veinlings + 2–4 Thorn Dancers
B. 18–25 Veinlings
C. 14–19 Veinlings + 3–5 Thorn Dancers

Creature specialists may receive a small roster-flavor adjustment only if total starting army value stays inside the faction band.

## Anti-reroll rule
Expected starting army value across all 16 regular heroes should stay within roughly ±10% before specialty value. No hero becomes the obvious tavern purchase solely because its army roll is materially richer.

## First-week economy test
Measure:
- resources remaining after each dwelling path
- whether T4 can coexist with City Hall too easily
- earliest practical T5
- opportunity cost of Court of Veins
- whether Citadel + Scarlet Conservatory produces too much week-one growth
- whether Crystal pressure is excessive rather than merely meaningful
