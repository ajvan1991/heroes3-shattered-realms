# Faction Bible

## Shared design constraints
- Seven creature tiers, each with one standard upgrade.
- No direct reskins of existing Heroes III creatures.
- Every faction needs at least one exploitable strategic weakness.
- Special mechanics use trade-offs rather than unconditional bonuses.
- Town economy must be benchmarked against existing factions before release.
- Tier 7 power is paid for through growth, cost, speed, survivability or development cost.

## Crimson Court
**Fantasy:** Blood Elves; immortal aristocracy sustained by ritual blood magic.
**Visuals:** black marble, crimson crystal, pale moonlight, living bloodwood.
**Might:** Bloodlord.
**Magic:** Sanguine Seer.
**Mechanic:** Blood Rites — controlled sacrifice for temporary advantage.
**Weakness:** expensive elite units; abilities frequently require injury, sacrifice or setup.

Roster:
1. Veinling -> Bloodbound
2. Thorn Dancer -> Crimson Dancer
3. Gorewing -> Bloodwing
4. Hemomancer -> Vein Oracle
5. Scarlet Huntress -> Bloodstalker
6. Sanguine Noble -> Crimson Archon
7. Blood Phoenix -> Eternal Blood Phoenix

## Abyss
**Fantasy:** impossible life from the depths beneath reality.
**Might:** Deep Sovereign.
**Magic:** Voidcaller.
**Mechanic:** Corruption.
**Weakness:** expensive army and comparatively slow conventional development.

Roster:
1. Whisper -> Murmur
2. Hollowling -> Hollow One
3. Riftcrawler -> Riftstalker
4. Faceless -> Skinshifter
5. Abyssal Oracle -> Blind Prophet
6. Mawborn -> Worldeater
7. Leviathan -> Elder Leviathan

## Veil
**Fantasy:** the dream world leaking into reality.
**Might:** Lucid Warden.
**Magic:** Somnomancer.
**Mechanic:** Dream / Nightmare stance.
**Weakness:** lower raw physical damage; relies on positioning and control.

Roster:
1. Glimmer -> Moon Glimmer
2. Dreamcap -> Nightmare Cap
3. Lantern Hart -> Moon Hart
4. Reverie -> Lucid Reverie
5. Nightmare Weaver -> Dream Eater
6. Moon Shepherd -> Eclipse Shepherd
7. Oneirion -> Eternal Oneirion

## Hollow
**Fantasy:** a royal court trapped between life and death after its king cheated Death.
**Might:** Oathbreaker.
**Magic:** Pale Oracle.
**Mechanic:** Oaths — each benefit carries a drawback.
**Weakness:** conditional power and deliberate tempo penalties.

Roster:
1. Candlekin -> Wickborn
2. Maskbearer -> Hollow Mask
3. Bell Mourner -> Death Kneller
4. Black Rose Maiden -> Thorn Widow
5. Oathless Knight -> Forsworn Knight
6. Pale Procession -> Endless Procession
7. Empty King -> King Without a Name

## Starfall
**Fantasy:** mythic celestial beings born around a fallen heavenly object.
**Might:** Halo Warden.
**Magic:** Astral Hierophant.
**Mechanic:** Constellations / celestial alignments.
**Weakness:** high building costs and low growth among elite units.

Roster:
1. Starling -> Radiant Starling
2. Halo Keeper -> Halo Warden
3. Many-Eyed -> All-Seer
4. Astral Pilgrim -> Star Prophet
5. Seraphic Horror -> Thousand-Wing
6. Constellation -> Living Constellation
7. Empyrean -> Throne of Heaven
