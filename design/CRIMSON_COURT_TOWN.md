# Crimson Court — Town & Building Bible

## Town composition
A vertical black-marble court grown around the **First Bloodwood**, an immense living crimson tree. Construction should visibly transform the scene: branches grow, bridges appear, moonlit pools fill, ritual lanterns awaken and aristocratic wings expand.

## Core visual layers
1. distant pale moon and mountainous silhouette
2. First Bloodwood trunk/canopy
3. black-marble terraces
4. crimson crystal waterways / ritual channels
5. foreground thorn garden

## Creature dwellings
- T1 **Vein House** — ritual school carved into living roots
- T2 **Thorn Gallery** — dueling arcade over a thorn garden
- T3 **Gorewing Roost** — suspended crimson-glass nesting vault
- T4 **Hall of Veins** — divination chamber around branching blood channels
- T5 **Scarlet Lodge** — moonlit hunting court and trophy colonnade
- T6 **Sanguine Palace** — aristocratic upper court
- T7 **Heart Aviary** — open ritual crown around the Bloodwood's highest boughs

Each upgraded dwelling changes silhouette, not merely lighting.

## Unique buildings
### Court of Veins
Unlocks the Blood Rite interface. Rites must exchange a real cost for a bounded temporary benefit.

### Scarlet Conservatory
Supports growth/economy but must compete with another meaningful build priority.

### Hall of the First Chalice
Faction lore / controlled hero benefit; no permanent free-stat farming.

### Moonlit Menagerie
Creature-support structure with narrow faction-specific effect.

### Thorn Tribunal
Defensive town identity; supports siege without simply copying Castle/Fortress bonuses.

### Red Moon Observatory
Magic-oriented structure tied to Sanguine Seers.

## Grail — Heart of the Red Moon
The Bloodwood flowers beneath a permanent red lunar halo. Grail effect must be powerful but remain within base-game Grail expectations; no global combat dominance.

## Animation plan
Town screen requires subtle looped life:
- Bloodwood branches sway independently.
- ritual channels pulse slowly, never like neon machinery.
- distant moonlight moves across upper marble.
- Gorewings occasionally cross the background after their dwelling exists.
- T7 dwelling adds sparse crimson-leaf motes.
- Grail adds a restrained lunar halo and flowering canopy.

The city must remain readable and painterly in classic Heroes III composition.
