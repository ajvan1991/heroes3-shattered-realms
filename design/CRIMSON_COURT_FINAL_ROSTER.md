# Crimson Court — Final Regular Hero Roster

Crimson Court follows the classic town pattern: **16 regular heroes: 8 Bloodlords (Might) + 8 Sanguine Seers (Magic).**

## Bloodlords
1. **Vaelor — Veinling/Bloodbound specialist.** Initiate-master; improves their Attack/Defense by classic level-scaled creature-specialist rules.
2. **Seris — Thorn Dancer/Crimson Dancer specialist.** Duelist; standard creature-specialist scaling.
3. **Khaeren — Gorewing/Bloodwing specialist.** Roostmaster; standard creature-specialist scaling.
4. **Maelira — Scarlet Huntress/Bloodstalker specialist.** Hunter; standard creature-specialist scaling.
5. **Othrys — Sanguine Noble/Crimson Archon specialist.** Aristocratic commander; standard creature-specialist scaling.
6. **Rhaevan — Blood Phoenix/Eternal Blood Phoenix specialist.** Conservative T7 specialty scaling; never adds extra rebirth charges.
7. **Thalia Veyn — Offense specialist.** Improves Offense in the familiar Heroes III manner.
8. **Ilyr Thorn — Blood Command specialist.** Improves tactical threshold behavior of the class passive without raising its maximum permanent raw-stat ceiling.

## Sanguine Seers
1. **Aveline — Hemomancer/Vein Oracle specialist.** Standard creature-specialist scaling.
2. **Sevrin — Blood spell specialist.** Improves one signature blood spell after the spell list is finalized.
3. **Miraleth — Blood Rite specialist.** Improves one Rite while respecting its mandatory minimum sacrifice.
4. **Caelis — Intelligence specialist.**
5. **Vespera — Sorcery specialist.**
6. **Elyss Vane — Mysticism specialist.**
7. **Saereth — Quarry Mark specialist.** Improves bounded utility/duration, never multiplicative infinite damage.
8. **Lysandra Noct — Crimson Divination specialist.** Improves the next-spell tactical payoff rather than bypassing sacrifice.

## Reserved campaign / special hero concepts
Corvane, Nerezza, Orlanna and Morcant are retained outside the regular tavern roster for later campaign/event use.

## Roster balance
- Regular tavern pool remains 8 + 8.
- Creature specialists cover multiple army tiers without forcing every tier to have one.
- T7 specialty is deliberately conservative.
- Existing-skill specialists keep the faction legible to veteran Heroes III players.
- Faction-mechanic specialists provide novelty but cannot remove the core tradeoff.
