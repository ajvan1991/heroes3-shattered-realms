## Artifact balance rules
- Treasure/minor artifacts use narrow, understandable effects.
- Major/relic artifacts can alter tactics but must not invalidate entire schools of play.
- Combination artifacts require meaningful slot/opportunity cost.
- Charged effects are preferred for powerful battle-changing abilities.
- No artifact grants an unconditional extra turn loop, permanent hard crowd-control loop or uncapped resurrection.

## Reward design
Every structure belongs to one of five reward patterns:
- **Choice:** two modest rewards with different strategic value.
- **Risk/reward:** fight or pay a cost for a better reward.
- **Delayed:** mark/quest-like state that pays later.
- **Conversion:** exchange one resource category for another at bounded efficiency.
- **Exploration:** reveal, movement, portal or information utility.

No structure should be a repeatable infinite-resource engine.
