# Crimson Court — Creature Production Bible

## Visual language
Blood Elves are elegant, predatory and ritualistic rather than demonic. Silhouettes use long ceremonial shapes, thorn motifs, blackened silver, dark crimson cloth, pale skin and living bloodwood. Avoid direct visual duplication of existing elves, vampires, harpies, phoenixes or demons.

Every creature requires:
- battle model/sprite direction
- adventure-map sprite direction where applicable
- large/small recruitment icons
- idle, move, attack, hit, defend and death animation plan
- upgrade silhouette differentiation
- concise in-game description
- ability VFX notes
- sound direction

## T1 — Veinling -> Bloodbound
**Description:** Young court thralls who have survived their first Blood Rite. Pain sharpens rather than breaks them.
**Silhouette:** slim ritual initiate, hooked bloodwood blade, asymmetric mantle.
**Upgrade:** ritual scar patterns glow faintly; larger thorn blade; half-mask.
**Animation:** nervous breathing idle; low stalking walk; quick diagonal slash; hit recoils then deliberately straightens.
**Passive — Bloodied:** below 50% current stack HP, gains a small Attack bonus. No stacking.
**Role:** cheap melee whose efficiency rises when damaged, but remains fragile.

## T2 — Thorn Dancer -> Crimson Dancer
**Description:** Duelists trained among living thorn galleries where a single misplaced step means death.
**Silhouette:** flowing duelist, paired crescent-thorn blades; no bow.
**Upgrade:** longer red-black ribbons and thorn crown; sharper spinning silhouette.
**Animation:** poised idle footwork; circular attack; upgrade's first offensive strike uses a passing cut.
**Passive:** mobility/dueling. Upgrade may avoid retaliation on its first attack of its own turn only.
**Role:** precision melee; low durability prevents free trading.

## T3 — Gorewing -> Bloodwing
**Description:** Nocturnal court predators bound by oath rather than domestication, with membrane wings veined like crimson glass.
**Silhouette:** original batlike-avian mythic predator; four grasping limbs, narrow skull crest. Avoid standard bat/harpy silhouette.
**Upgrade:** broader translucent wing membranes and ceremonial collar.
**Animation:** hanging/low-hover idle; diving claw attack; feeding recovery after kill.
**Passive:** flying. Upgrade restores a capped amount of surviving-stack HP after dealing lethal damage; cannot freely resurrect casualties.
**Role:** mobile finisher.

## T4 — Hemomancer -> Vein Oracle
**Description:** Blood readers who divine futures from living currents and pay for prophecy with their own vitality.
**Silhouette:** robed elf mage with floating ritual needles and liquid-red sigils; no staff.
**Upgrade:** blindfold/vein crown; orbiting chalice fragments.
**Animation:** liquid sigils circulate during idle; spell cast draws a small red glyph from caster to target.
**Passive:** limited caster; stronger effects have HP/resource cost.
**Role:** tactical utility with deliberately weak physical profile.

## T5 — Scarlet Huntress -> Bloodstalker
**Description:** Noble hunters who mark a quarry with a ritual wound and pursue it beyond ordinary endurance.
**Silhouette:** tall huntress with recurved bloodwood launcher distinct from classic elven bow; long mantle.
**Upgrade:** masked face, segmented crimson weapon, stronger stalking posture.
**Animation:** controlled ranged release; mark VFX is a small thorn-ring, not a giant glow.
**Passive — Quarry Mark:** attacks mark a target for bounded follow-up pressure; no multiplicative infinite stacking.
**Role:** premium ranged specialist vulnerable in melee.

## T6 — Sanguine Noble -> Crimson Archon
**Description:** Ancient aristocrats whose presence binds the court together as surely as blood binds a body.
**Silhouette:** armored noble with floating cloak panels and ceremonial glaive; unmistakably living elf, not vampire.
**Upgrade:** crown-like back ornament, heavier black-silver plate, red crystal veins.
**Animation:** restrained regal idle; heavy sweeping attack; aura pulses subtly.
**Passive:** conservative sustain/support aura with limited radius.
**Role:** expensive anchor.

## T7 — Blood Phoenix -> Eternal Blood Phoenix
**Description:** A forbidden court miracle: not a firebird, but a living storm of crimson leaves, blood-light and featherlike crystal reborn around a ritual heart.
**Silhouette:** deliberately distinct from Conflux Phoenix — elongated crown, four sweeping crystal-leaf wings, dark core, crimson rather than flame body.
**Upgrade:** additional outer wing arc and visible ritual-heart halo.
**Animation:** body continuously sheds leaf/crystal motes; attack collapses into a crimson arc and reforms; death implodes rather than burns.
**Passive:** fast apex. Upgrade has one tightly capped rebirth per battle.
**Role:** high-risk mobility T7; lower raw durability than tank-oriented T7s.

## Production constraint
No final asset is approved if its silhouette can be mistaken at combat zoom for an existing Heroes III creature.
