# Lore & Description Style

Shattered Realms text should sound like Heroes III-era fantasy: concise, evocative and readable. Avoid modern slang, excessive cosmic jargon and paragraphs of exposition inside gameplay tooltips.

## Creature descriptions
Target: 1-3 sentences. Establish origin, behavior and one memorable detail. Do not merely restate the ability.

## Building descriptions
Explain what the place is first, then its gameplay function.

## Hero biographies
Target: short paragraph. Give motive, reputation and faction relationship. Avoid chosen-one repetition.

## Ability names
Prefer concrete mythic names: Bloodied, Quarry Mark, Last Oath, Dreamstep. Avoid MMO-style names such as Ultimate Blood DPS Aura.

## Tone by faction
- Crimson Court: aristocratic, ritualistic, predatory.
- Abyss: ancient, unknowable, maritime/eldritch.
- Veil: dreamlike, beautiful, unsettling.
- Hollow: mournful, courtly, oath-bound.
- Starfall: sacred, celestial, awe-inspiring rather than technological.
